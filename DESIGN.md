# Design Context: Prerana

## Color Strategy: Committed
The landing page uses a Neutral background (FDFCFB) to let the individual bento cards carry the brand identity.

| Card | Primary | Background | Text |
|------|---------|------------|------|
| Krea | #EA580C (Orange-600) | #FFFAF0 | Slate-900 |
| Vanta | #FFFFFF | #050505 | White |
| Selaras | #94A3B8 | White/40 | Slate-800 |
| Hero | #EA580C | White | Slate-900 |

## Typography
- **Heading**: Serif-style (italic accents) for a "high-end portfolio" feel.
- **Body**: Inter or Montserrat for legibility.
- **Hierarchy**: Extreme scale contrast in the hero (7xl+) down to minute tracking-widest uppercase labels.

## Elevation & Spacing
- **Layout**: Bento Grid Showcase.
- **Radius**: Large, friendly corners (2.5rem).
- **Shadows**: Subtle, refined shadows for depth without clutter.

## Key Components
- **Bento Card**: Rounded containers with isolated themes.
- **Interactive Badge**: Capsule-shaped labels with icons.
- **CTA**: Rounded-full buttons with high weight.
