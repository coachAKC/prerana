# Product Context: Prerana

## Users
- **Solo Professionals & Freelancers**: Looking for high-performance, minimalist tools like Vanta.
- **Trainers & Educators**: Needing engagement-boosting platforms like Krea.
- **Enterprise Organizations**: Requiring secure document management like Selaras.

## Brand & Tone
- **Name**: Prerana (Sanskrit for "Inspiration").
- **Mission**: "We solve People Problems."
- **Tone**: Professional yet approachable, premium, high-energy (Krea), minimalist & high-performance (Vanta).
- **Core Value**: Removing friction from human effort.

## Strategic Principles
1. **People First**: Solve the human friction, not just the technical problem.
2. **Professional Instruments**: Build tools that feel like high-end instruments, not just "apps".
3. **Isolated Aesthetics**: Each product has its own clear visual "mood" (Claymorphic Krea vs. Dark/Stealth Vanta).
